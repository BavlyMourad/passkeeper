# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

PassKeeper is designed to keep your data on your device, not on our servers. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

---

## What We Collect

Nothing.

PassKeeper does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. We do not use:

- Analytics
- Tracking technologies
- Advertising identifiers
- User accounts
- Remote databases

Your data stays under your control.

---

## Where Your Data Lives

All passwords, categories, and application settings are stored **locally on your device**.

Before any password is written to local storage, it is encrypted using **AES-256-GCM** with a unique encryption key derived from your PIN.

Because all data remains on your device, PassKeeper cannot access, read, recover, or share your passwords.

---

## Biometric Authentication

If you enable fingerprint or face authentication, the feature is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

PassKeeper:

- Does not receive biometric information
- Does not store biometric data
- Does not transmit biometric data

Authentication is performed securely by your device.

---

## Backups

PassKeeper does not automatically back up your data.

If your device backs up application data to a cloud service (such as your device manufacturer's backup service), that backup is governed by the encryption, security, and privacy policies of that cloud provider—not by PassKeeper.

---

## Deleting Your Data

All application data is stored locally.

Uninstalling PassKeeper permanently removes all locally stored passwords and settings from your device.

Since PassKeeper never stores a copy of your data on external servers, there is nothing to delete from our systems.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made:

- The **"Last updated"** date at the top of this document will be revised.
- If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will notify you within the app.
